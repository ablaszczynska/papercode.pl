AI Routine Creation

The Application offers an optional feature that creates a skincare routine for you using an artificial intelligence model. This feature is the only part of the Application that sends information about your skin off your device, and it is never used without your explicit agreement.

Nothing is sent until you agree. The first time you ask for a routine to be created, the Application shows you a consent screen describing what will be sent. Nothing leaves your device unless you accept it. If you decline, the feature simply does not run, and the rest of the Application is unaffected. If this policy or the data being sent changes materially, you will be asked again rather than carried over on your earlier agreement.

What is sent. Only the information needed to produce a routine:

  • The skin type you selected
  • The skin concerns you selected
  • The optional free-text note you write, if you write one
  • A random identifier generated on your device, used only to limit how many routines a single installation can request per day

Your name, email address, contacts, photos, precise location and the contents of your existing routines are not sent. The Application has no user accounts, so none of this is connected to an identity.

Who processes it. The request is sent to a server operated by the Service Provider, hosted on Google Cloud in the European Union (region europe-west1), which then calls Google’s Gemini model. The request is not made directly from your device to Google.

Your answers are not used to train AI models. The Service Provider uses a paid Google Gemini plan, under which Google does not use submitted prompts or generated responses to improve or train its models.

Retention. The Service Provider’s server does not store your answers. Each request is handled and discarded; nothing about your skin is kept on the server after the routine has been generated. The routine itself is saved only on your device. Google processes the request under its own terms as an independent data controller for abuse-prevention purposes; see the links below.

Legal basis and your rights. Information about your skin may constitute health data, which is special-category personal data under Article 9 of the GDPR. The Service Provider processes it solely on the basis of your explicit consent (Article 9(2)(a)). You may withdraw that consent at any time by simply not using the feature; withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out beforehand, and routines already saved remain on your device. You may also contact skincareplus@papercode.pl to exercise your rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability and objection.

This feature does not provide medical advice. Routines it generates are general guidance only. They are not a diagnosis, not a treatment plan, and not a substitute for advice from a dermatologist or other qualified healthcare professional.
