You open the fridge. You forget why.

Midnight Fridge is a memory game where your phone is the fridge. The magnets on the door are what you came for. Remember them, open the fridge, and find exactly those things among everything else. Shut it, and the door shows how you did. One more thing to remember each round, up to twenty, with three lives.

On iPhone Duo the phone itself is the fridge: the list is on the outer screen, and unfolding opens the door. On other iPhones, pull the handle to swing the door open.

Six fridges to open: the morning after a party, the family's, the flatmates', Grandma's, the office's, and one full of vegetables nobody's eating.

No ads, no accounts, no in-app purchases and no tracking. It never goes online, and your progress stays on your phone.
