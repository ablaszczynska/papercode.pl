**Privacy Policy**

This privacy policy applies to the Focal app (hereby referred to as "Application") for mobile devices that was created by Paper Code Adrianna Blaszczynska (hereby referred to as "Service Provider") as a Commercial service. This service is intended for use "AS IS".

**The short version**

Focal does not collect, store, or share any personal data. Everything you read in Focal stays on your device.

**Information Collection and Use**

The Application does not collect any information. In particular:

*   The Application has no user accounts and no registration.
*   The texts and articles you add to the Application are stored only locally on your device. They are never uploaded to the Service Provider or to any third party.
*   Your reading preferences (font, theme, focus strength) are stored only locally on your device.
*   The Application contains no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting, and no tracking of any kind.
*   The Application does not access your location.

**Importing articles from the web**

When you import an article from a link, the Application downloads that web page directly from your device, in the same way a web browser would. The website you are importing from will see a standard web request (including your IP address), governed by that website's own privacy policy. No intermediary server operated by the Service Provider is involved, and the downloaded article is stored only on your device.

**Purchases**

Focal Premium subscriptions are processed entirely by Apple through your App Store account. The Service Provider does not receive or store your name, payment details, or any other billing information. For details, see [Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

**External services you choose to open**

The Application contains an optional "Request a feature" link that opens the Service Provider's public feedback board (Featurebase) in an in-app browser. Anything you post there is governed by [Featurebase's Privacy Policy](https://www.featurebase.app/privacy-policy). The Application never sends anything to this service on its own.

**Data Retention and Deletion**

Because all data is stored only on your device, you are in full control of it. You can delete individual saved texts inside the Application at any time, and you can remove all Application data by uninstalling the Application.

**Children**

The Application does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13.

**Security**

Your content never leaves your device, which is the strongest safeguard available. The Application relies on the security mechanisms of your device's operating system.

**Changes**

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time for any reason. The Service Provider will notify you of any changes to the Privacy Policy by updating this page with the new Privacy Policy. You are advised to consult this Privacy Policy regularly for any changes, as continued use is deemed approval of all changes.

This privacy policy is effective as of 2026-07-22

If you have any questions regarding privacy while using the Application, or have questions about the practices, please contact the Service Provider via email at focal@papercode.pl.
